Read the product boundary ↗

The definition is part of the customer story.

“Outstanding,” “open,” and “active” carry decisions a schema cannot settle. Review those rules once, keep the approved context, and refresh the underlying facts. Save a brief before the next review; when the identity or definition changes, the comparison makes that change explicit.
